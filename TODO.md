# Study Jarvis — Outcomes

- [x] **AI conversation and study help** — “Ask open-ended questions and have natural conversations with an AI assistant.” “Get student-focused study help, including explanations and support with learning topics.” The assistant provides useful, readable answers and study-oriented help. The chat and study-help path is implemented; no signed-in live provider conversation was exercised in this validation.
- [x] **Voice-first communication** — “Take voice commands” and “make it talk back too”; the assistant opens in voice mode, a student records a spoken request and stops the recording to send it automatically, and assistant replies are spoken by default. Spoken replies can be muted, stopped, or replayed. Text chat is an explicit fallback, and microphone/transcription errors explain how to switch to text. Live microphone, transcription, and audible playback still require a browser/device test.
- [x] **Personal in-app schedule** — “Organize classes, assignments, exams, and reminders in a personal schedule.” A signed-in student can create, review, edit, complete, and delete these schedule items; their records persist and are isolated to their account. Protected, account-scoped CRUD and the schedule table are present; no signed-in CRUD round-trip was exercised in this validation.
- [x] **Conversational schedule management** — “Review upcoming schedule items and manage them through conversational or voice requests.” The assistant can use the student’s own schedule to answer upcoming-item questions and carry out validated requests to add, update, complete, or remove schedule items. The account-scoped context and validated schedule actions are implemented; model interpretation and a live tool-action round-trip were not exercised in this validation.
- [x] **Responsive Paper Signal experience** — Apply the selected warm, notebook-inspired Paper Signal direction with approachable editorial typography and an earthy ink/clay/oat palette; keep chat and schedule usable on mobile and desktop. Responsive layouts, visible touch controls, and keyboard focus styling are implemented.

Validation completed: TypeScript check, test suite (7 tests), production build, and HTTP smoke checks for the app, health endpoint, and route manifest. The build still reports a non-fatal large-bundle warning. No live microphone, transcription, or audible playback round-trip has been tested.

External calendar synchronization and background push notifications are not part of the confirmed scope.
